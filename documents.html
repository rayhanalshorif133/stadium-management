Slide 02 • Executive Summary

Current Operational Challenges & Our Proposed Solution

02 / 10
Key Operational Pain Points
  • Fragmented Shop Visibility: Difficult to track real-time occupancy, vacant units, and shops under repair across Blocks A, B, C, and D.
  • Slow Manual Billing & Reconciliation: Combining base rent with electricity, water, and service charges manually delays collection and receipting.
  • Lease Expiry & Sub-Letting Risks: Lack of automated expiry alerts and digital allocation history leads to delayed renewals and compliance gaps.
  • Unstructured Maintenance & Notices: Match-day closures and urgent shutter/electrical repair complaints lack centralized accountability.
Strategic Value of the Proposed Portal
  • Single Source of Truth: Executive dashboard and interactive 100-shop floor grid give instant clarity on occupancy and revenue health.
  • Automated Invoicing & Official Receipts: Generate monthly invoices in seconds, track bKash/Bank/Cash payments, and print tamper-proof receipts.
  • Proactive Lease & KYC Control: Automated 15 & 30-day renewal alerts, verified NID/Trade License records, and refundable trust fund oversight.
  • Data-Driven Revenue Growth: Vacancy loss analytics pinpoint underperforming blocks and recommend pricing strategies to recover lost rent.
Business Goal: Eliminate manual leakage, accelerate cash flow, and achieve 100% audit readiness Executive Proposal
