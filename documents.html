Slide 02 • Overview

Why Manage Everything Through One Automated App?

02 / 08
Without Automation
  • Managing many shops across the whole stadium in paper registers is slow and confusing.
  • Hard to instantly see how many shops paid rent and how many are still due.
  • Monthly electricity, water, and gas bills have to be calculated manually one by one.
  • Tenant details, shop history, and lease expiry dates can easily get lost.
With Our Proposed App
  • Allocate & Manage Every Shop: See all stadium shops in one list & visual map.
  • Automated Billing & Auto Invoice: Generate monthly invoices and collect rent with receipts in 1 click.
  • Utility Automation: Electricity, water, and gas bills are added automatically every month.
  • Live Owner Observation: Check paid vs. due shops, tenant info, and full shop history anytime.
Built specifically for a Stadium Owner managing multiple shops Simple & Automated
